Architectural practice

How I Approach Architecture

Technology choices should follow the problem, not define it.

  1. 01

    Understand the Problem

    What are we trying to achieve, for whom, and under which constraints?

  2. 02

    Define the Boundaries

    What belongs in the solution, and what should remain outside the first release?

  3. 03

    Evaluate Architectural Choices

    Which approaches fit the requirements, and what tradeoffs do they introduce?

  4. 04

    Direct Implementation

    Translate requirements into architecture, interfaces, acceptance criteria, and manageable milestones.

  5. 05

    Validate and Evolve

    A working implementation is the beginning of evaluation, not necessarily the end of delivery.

Technology becomes valuable when we understand not only what it can do, but why and how it should be applied.
