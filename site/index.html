About Me

I'm an intelligent automation specialist with more than ten years of experience in IT and extensive experience designing and delivering enterprise automation solutions.

My professional journey has taken me from IT service management through process automation and solution design to exploring how AI can extend the capabilities of traditional automation.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of AI Architecture, intelligent automation, and product thinking: understanding business problems, designing appropriate solutions, evaluating architectural tradeoffs, and translating requirements into practical outcomes.

Outside my professional work, I develop independent projects as a way to explore emerging technologies and challenge my architectural thinking. Wordy and BoomArena are two examples of this approach.

Both were developed through AI-assisted implementation, with my contribution focused on product vision, architectural direction, requirements, validation, and iterative delivery.

I believe technology becomes valuable when we understand not only what it can do, but why and how it should be applied.
